Featured Initiative

Fairfax Parks & Recreation Renewal

Spring 2026 City-Wide

Council members Peterson and Hall, alongside Councilmember McQuillen, have united behind an ambitious plan to revitalize Fairfax's parks and recreation facilities. The initiative includes new playground equipment at Rotary Park, restored trails at Blenheim Forest, and extended community center hours to serve working families.
